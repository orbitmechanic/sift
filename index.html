    A door from Pinterest

    Bring your boards over

    Sift cannot read Pinterest directly, so a small bookmarklet does the reading inside your Pinterest tab and hands the boards to Sift. Your Pinterest password never touches Sift.

    1

    Name the account

    Use the name in pinterest.com/name/.

    2

    Install the importer

    Drag this button to your bookmarks bar.

    Sift Pinterest Importer

    If the bar is hidden, press Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac.

    3

    Run it on Pinterest

    Open Pinterest, sign in for private boards and the fullest public feeds, then click the importer in your bookmarks bar. Sift opens with a preview before anything is saved.

    Private boards appear only when the username belongs to the account signed in to Pinterest in this browser. Pinterest may limit anonymous sessions to a preview of each board. Imports merge into boards with the same name, and duplicate links are skipped.

    The fine print, in plain words

    Private by architecture

    Sift has no account system, no analytics, no cookies, and no server of its own. There is nothing to monetize, so there are no ads to serve.

    • Your boards live here. Names, image links, and a little metadata are stored in this browser's localStorage. No image files are downloaded into your archive, and no data is sent to us.
    • Searches go straight to the source. Your query is sent directly from your browser to Openverse, with Wikimedia Commons as a fallback. Openverse is run by WordPress; their privacy policy applies to those requests.
    • No search history is stored. Sift keeps the current search in memory only. Reload the page and it is gone.
    • Image hosts see image requests. This page uses no-referrer, so hosts do not receive the page you came from, but they can still see your IP address when an image loads.
    • Pinterest import stays in your browser. The optional importer is a bookmarklet that runs inside your Pinterest tab, reads the boards your browser session can see, and passes them directly to Sift. Pinterest credentials are never shared with Sift, and nothing passes through a third-party server.
    • Web image capture is deliberate. Search engines do not allow this static page to read their results. The optional absorber runs only when you click it on an image-search tab, reads the thumbnails already displayed there, and writes their links to this browser's display buffer. It does not read cookies or send anything to a server.
    • You own the escape hatch. Export your boards as JSON at any time. Clearing this site's browser storage removes them permanently.

    Openverse and Wikimedia Commons return openly licensed images. Check the license on each original source before reusing an image.
